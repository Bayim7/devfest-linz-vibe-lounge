How It Works

Four steps to glory. No pressure, maximum chaos.

1. Get Credits

Redeem your provided Google Cloud budget to access the Gemini API.

2. Pick a Tier

Choose from Low-Code, Intermediate, or Hard Mode challenges.

3. The Twist

At minute 20, the alarm sounds! Musical Keyboards: swap laptops and finish your neighbor's code.

4. Demo Time

You have 60 seconds to pitch whatever Frankenstein app you ended up with.

Choose Your Challenge

Whether you're a prompt engineering wizard or a seasoned full-stack developer, there is a track for you.

Portfolio with AI

Swap Twist:

Multiplayer AI Game
